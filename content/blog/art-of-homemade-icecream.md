There's a world of difference between commercial ice cream and what comes out of our small-batch churner. Here's why artisanal ice cream is worth every bite.

## Real Ingredients, Real Flavour

We start with fresh cream, whole milk, and real sugar. Our flavours come from actual fruits, nuts, and spices, not artificial extracts. When we say "mango," we mean Alphonso mangoes hand-selected at peak ripeness.

## The Churn

Commercial ice cream is pumped full of air (called "overrun") to increase volume cheaply. Our ice cream has minimal overrun, which means every spoonful is denser, creamier, and more intensely flavoured.

## Small Batches, Big Care

We make each flavour in batches of no more than five litres. This lets us control every variable (temperature, timing, texture), ensuring consistency that mass production simply cannot match.

## Our Favourite Flavours

From classic Belgian chocolate and Madagascar vanilla to desi-inspired paan kulfi and filter coffee, our menu changes with the seasons. Follow us on Instagram to catch our latest creations before they sell out.

## Made to Order

Unlike store-bought ice cream that might sit in a freezer for months, our ice cream is made to order and delivered fresh. That's the Velvet Oven difference.
